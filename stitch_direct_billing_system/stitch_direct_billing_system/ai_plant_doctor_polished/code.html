Based on the visual evidence, your plant is likely suffering from Early Blight (Alternaria solani).

Diagnosis Details

Concentric rings in dark spots and yellowing of surrounding tissue are classic signs. This usually spreads from lower leaves upwards.

Treatment Plan

  • Remove and destroy infected lower leaves.
  • Avoid overhead watering to keep foliage dry.
  • Apply a copper-based organic fungicide.
